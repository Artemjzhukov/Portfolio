- `get_pattern(id)` — retrieve a pattern by stable ID
- `list_patterns(category, ce_level)` — list patterns filtered by category/level
- `normalize_pattern_id(raw)` — normalize ID strings with alias mapping
- `build_patterns_prompt(patterns, student_level)` — build LLM prompt segment referencing selected patterns

### Integration with lesson generation:

- Unknown/level-mismatched pattern IDs are silently skipped (no fabrication)

## 2. First-month curriculum (4-week pilot)

### Week 1 — Building sentences and describing experience
**Focus**:
- Word order.
- Present Simple / Present Continuous.
- Past Simple / Present Perfect.
- Basic articles in context.

**Speaking**:
- Self-introduction.
- Current responsibilities.
- Professional background.
- Previous projects and achievements.

**Expected outcome**:
A coherent 2–3 minute self-introduction without memorizing every sentence.

### Week 2 — Explaining experience and processes
**Focus**:
- Present Perfect Continuous.
- Past Perfect.
- Passive Voice.
- Relative Clauses.

**Speaking**:
- Describe a project.
- Explain a process.
- Describe a business problem.
- Explain the solution and its results.

**Expected outcome**:
A structured 3–5 minute explanation of a professional project.

### Week 3 — Reasoning and argumentation
**Focus**:
- Gerund / Infinitive.
- Conditionals.
- Modal verbs.
- Linking words.
- Complex sentences.

**Speaking**:
- Explain decisions.
- Compare alternative approaches.
- Discuss hypothetical situations.
- Justify conclusions.

**Expected outcome**:
Ability to explain reasoning and respond to follow-up questions.

### Week 4 — Integration and spontaneous speaking
**Focus**:
- Recycle previously learned patterns.
- Combine multiple grammatical constructions.
- Improve accuracy and sentence complexity.

**Speaking**:
- Unexpected questions.
- Professional discussion.
- Short interview scenarios.
- Spontaneous answers without sentence templates.

**Expected outcome**:
Combine grammar, vocabulary and functional templates in relatively independent speech.

## 3. Pattern progression (three stages)
1. **Recognition** — identify the correct construction; complete a missing part; recognize in context.
2. **Controlled production** — build sentences with support; replace sentence slots; produce independent examples.
3. **Free production** — use the pattern in a new context without an explicit template; answer unexpected questions; apply in conversational/professional context.

Mastery is not equated with correctly answering one multiple-choice question; free production is the goal.

## 4. Integration with lesson generation

- Only patterns matching the student's level are included
- Existing lesson-generation prompts (4-phase scaffolding) can reference pattern IDs
- Cached lessons are preserved; new lessons can incorporate patterns
